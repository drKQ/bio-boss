Bio Boss Privacy Policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

Bio Boss is a free study game for anatomy, physiology, medical terminology and lab science. It collects no personal information.

What we collect

Bio Boss has no account, no sign in, no ads and no tracking across other apps or websites. To improve the questions and games, the app sends anonymous usage data: which questions are answered right or wrong, which games are played and their scores, how long a session lasts, and the app version. It never sends your name, the player name you type, your email, your contacts, your location or anything that identifies you.

Each install gets a random code that is scrambled (hashed) on your iPhone before anything is sent, so we cannot tell who you are. The data is processed by TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service in the European Union, and is used only to improve Bio Boss.

You can turn this off any time: Me tab, Settings, "Share anonymous usage data."

Where your progress lives

Your level, coins, scores and study progress are saved on your iPhone. If you use iCloud, a copy is also kept in your own iCloud account (Apple's key-value storage), so your progress comes back on a new iPhone signed in to the same Apple ID. That copy belongs to you; we cannot see it.

The player name you type on the title screen stays on your iPhone and in your iCloud copy. It is never sent to us.

Classes, reports and feedback

If you join a class, the class code (for example a course number) is included with the anonymous usage data so the teacher can see how a class is doing overall. The real name you type when joining stays on your iPhone. It only appears on the progress report picture that you choose to save and turn in yourself, for example on Canvas.

The "Tell Dr. KQ" button opens your own email app with a message you write and send yourself. Challenge codes you send to friends contain a subject, a number and your score, and go only where you send them.

Game Center, reminders and widgets

If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center under your Game Center name, as described in Apple's privacy policy. Reminders and widgets are created on your iPhone; nothing about them is sent to us.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove it from your iPhone. You can also erase game progress on the Me tab, and study progress at the bottom of each course. To remove the iCloud copy, turn off iCloud for Bio Boss in Settings, or delete its data under Settings, your name, iCloud, Manage Storage.

Children

Bio Boss is made for college students. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.
