Bio Boss Privacy Policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

Bio Boss is a free study game for anatomy, physiology and medical terminology. It does not collect any data about you.

What we collect

Nothing. Bio Boss has no account, no sign in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We never receive your name, your answers or your progress.

Where your progress lives

Your level, coins, scores and study progress are saved on your iPhone. If you use iCloud, a copy is also kept in your own iCloud account (Apple's key-value storage), so your progress comes back on a new iPhone signed in to the same Apple ID. That copy belongs to you; we cannot see it.

The player name you type on the title screen stays on your iPhone and in your iCloud copy. It is never sent to us.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove it from your iPhone. You can also erase game progress on the Me tab, and study progress at the bottom of each course. To remove the iCloud copy, turn off iCloud for Bio Boss in Settings, or delete its data under Settings, your name, iCloud, Manage Storage.

Children

Bio Boss is made for college students. It collects no data from anyone, of any age.
