Bio Boss

A&P study game for iPhone

Bio Boss turns anatomy, physiology and medical terminology into a video game: worlds and bosses, 20 arcade games, a hero to dress and a castle to defend. Every question was written by a college instructor for his own students, and every wrong answer explains why it is wrong.

Help

My progress is missing on a new iPhone. Progress comes back from your own iCloud. Sign in to the same Apple ID and make sure iCloud is on for Bio Boss in Settings, then open the app again.

The sounds are too loud or too quiet. Game sounds follow the ring/silent switch, and you can turn them off on the Me tab. Pronunciations in Medical Terms always play.

I found a mistake in a question. Please email the question and what you think is wrong. Every report gets read.
